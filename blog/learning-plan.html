Te Whare Tapa Whā: Are any of the walls of my whare in need of strengthening? How might I do this?

Due to the time pressures of the course, particularly later in the week, I have found myself eating less healthily. I could try and address this by preparing some meals earlier in the week when I have more time and energy.

What are some of my key needs at the moment and are there some strategies I could try for meeting these needs?

Generally I feel like my energy levels have been pretty good and I have remained enthusiastic about the course and do not feel burned out. I am however feeling a little more tired toward after Friday, often resulting in me having to decompress that evening e.g. by doing something fun and stress free. I would like to try and make the group project a little less stressful for myself and my teammates, possibly by reducing the scope of the MVP.

What does my stress profile look like? What does stress look like externally (how might others tell)? What does it feel like internally (how might you know)? What do you need when you notice you are stressed?

When I am stressed, I can feel my thoughts speed up and this can be seen by others as me talking/ moving faster than usual. Sometimes others can see stress/ panic on my face, other times I have found that others perceive me as being calm in a crisis. The determining factor appears to be how much emotional/ physical reserve I have on that day, which is usually influenced by my energy levels and how tired or run down I am feeling.

What's a wellbeing experiment I can try over the next few weeks?

I want to try and reduce stress towards the end of the week, for example by preparing meals but also by trying not to put too much on my plate for the final day of the group project. i.e. try not to start any new features on the Friday, rather tidy up features we already have. I would like to also try and increase my exercise from 1 to 2 times per week.

Bootcamp Reflections

What is your long term goal and/or career pathway?

I am open to see what's out there and to keep an open mind! Currently my preferred directions are either full stack developer or game developer.

Reflection- I still see either full stack and/or game development as long term goals. I initially wondered whether I would enjoy backend coding as much as making games as I have done more of the latter in the past, however I was pleasantly surprised how much I enjoy backend coding and interacting with databases. On reflection, in addition to making games on unity, I have always enjoyed making an overcomplicated excel spreadsheets which is suppose is analogous to backend coding! I am definitely not as passionate about frontend coding and would probably not want to do a job long term where this was my focus, however would be happy to do frontend coding in the context of a full stack job.

What do you think your biggest strengths and limitations will be in Bootcamp?

So far I have felt very confident with JavaScript as it is similar to C# which is a language I am familiar with. I feel that I can pick up new concepts in JavaScript relatively easily as I can connect these to prior knowledge. My HTML skills are not as strong. I do think that I work well in a group, however previously I have always done coding alone and so I may be initially limited in my ability to work with others in this setting as I learn how to do this.

Reflection- Whilst I think this is still largely accurate, I have felt in the past few weeks where we have been introduced to new concepts such as react and knex that I am coming to the limits of any advantage my prior knowledge gave me. I feel I am picking up the new concepts relatively quickly, but it is certainly not as easy as it was with basic JS! I feel that in general I have worked well in a team, however I have had to work at ensuring I don't allow my enthusiasm to mean I talk over others and ensure that everyone in the team has equal voice.

What do you think your biggest non-technical challenge at Bootcamp will be?

I think I will have to work hard to maintain good energy levels for the nine weeks. I will have to make sure I give myself enough time to relax and to utilise mindfulness and my Te Whare Tapa Whā wellbeing plan.

Reflection- my energy levels have been pretty good during Bootcamp, notwithstanding 4 or 5 days where I developed a terrible cold! Looking at my Te Whare Tapa Whā wellbeing plan, I have been doing most of the activities I set myself- I have walked in nature and been in contact with friends and family each week, I have taken breaks and listened to the tuis in my garden and I have been able to talk quite a lot about my Cornish heritage during the course, in part due to my other course mates being very kind and inquisitive! Whilst I have not been very good at remembering to do progressive muscle relaxation/ breathing exercises before bed, I have been watching the mindfulness videos which are part of the course. I was pleasantly surprised how calming the mindfulness instructors voice is, as in the past I have always found led meditation sessions difficult as I can find listening to voices of other people who are trying to relax me sometimes grating!

What non-technical skills - human skills - would you like to see developed in yourself while at Dev Academy?

I would like to work on communication skills within the coding context. I would like for example to practice pair programming to work on 1 on 1 communication and working on articulating my thought process when I am approaching coding.

Reflection- I do feel that my communication during pared programming and in group projects has improved and feel more comfortable thinking out loud during points where I am attempting to solve a problem by "trying something". I still think I have some improvements to go in terms of ensuring clear communication and ensuring everyone is on the same page, has a clear understanding of the current task and all feel included, however I am hopeful that this will come in time.

What are your expectations from the Bootcamp team?

To offer help if I need it and to offer guidance If I encounter any interpersonal issues working within our group. I would like to work in person when possible and so I would like assistance if there is any issues with the workspace, or information on who to contact at the workspace if we have any issues.

Reflection- The bootcamp team have been very helpful, friendly and accommodating and quick to offer solutions when I have had a problem or question. I have been supported in coming in in person to the wellington base a few times each week. I have enjoyed coming in to work and have found being in a working environment can help e.g. with distractions, however as only one other person has thus far come in person to the wellington base, there has not been as many opportunities to work in person with other people as I had hoped.

What are your expectations of yourself on Bootcamp

I will work with other learners in a productive manner, try to give everyone a chance to speak and contribute. I will aim to work collaboratively with others so everyone can get ahead. I will be respectful of facilitators and will approach them with any concerns or questions. I will try to ask questions early to get the most clarity (after attempting to find any solutions myself!) If I have any issues with the course or with others I will try to talk to facilitators about these before they become more of a problem.

Reflection- I feel I have largely stuck to these expectations and where I have noticed that I have not been living up to them as much as I would like, I have taken active steps to address this. For example, actively checking in on myself when I feel my excitement may be impacting upon other peoples ability to express their views and giving them time to speak.
